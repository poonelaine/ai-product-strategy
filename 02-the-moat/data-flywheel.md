### Correction Loop - 5/5
**What you capture today:** Agent to research and map the external market signals with company policies.

**How it compounds:** User to review the mapping and correct the mapping if diagree. Agent to capture those changes and feedback to the model reasoning for next research.


### Preference Loop - 1/5
**What you capture today:** Agent to research and map the external market signals with company policies.

**How it compounds:** User to review and correct mapping if disagree. Changes are captured as training for reasoning, however, it is intended not to customize the reasoning to individual users, in order to create a standardized judgement across different users from the same persona.  


### Domain Context Loop - 5/5
**What you capture today:** Agent to rate a risk score and explain why it matters to our company.

**How it compounds:** User to review and overwrite the risk score and why it matters to our company, user has the professional and experienced business knowledge in assessment of new factories. The overwritten result will be captured and feedbacked to the model reasoning.

### Network Loop - 5/5
**What you capture today:** Agent to rate a risk score and explain why it matters to our company.

**How it compounds:**  The more input from user group, the more business domain knowledge could be capture, and the model reasoning could be trained across the user inputs from global offices.
